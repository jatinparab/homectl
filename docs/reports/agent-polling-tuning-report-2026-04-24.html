~Flat
  • As expected, agent-side activity increased after tightening intervals.
  • Manager process did not show material CPU/RSS change in this short window.
  • 192.168.1.101 showed the largest increase due to workload profile and low pre baseline in this sample window.

Caveats

  • This is a short pre/post window (~60s each) and not a full-day steady-state profile.
  • Sampling captures current workload; node-level app mix can skew absolute CPU deltas.
  • For high confidence, run a 12–24h follow-up and compare p95 CPU plus journal volume.

Recommendation

Keep sensor=2s and reconcile=5s enabled for now. If you observe sustained high CPU on specific nodes, tune to sensor=2s + reconcile=8s as a fallback.
